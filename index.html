Biography

Junfeng Jiang is an Associate Professor at the College of Artificial Intelligence and Automation, Hohai University. He serves as the Deputy Director of the Institute of Human–Environment Intelligent Interaction and Systems.

His research focuses on AI-driven medical treatment planning and execution, orthopedic surgical robotics, medical image analysis, and 3D computer graphics. His work has been published in leading venues including Medical Physics, ICCV, MICCAI, EuroGraphics, Computer-Aided Design, and Computers & Industry. In 2026, his group published a semantic edge-guided single-view vertebral registration method in Medical Physics and had FlowReg published at MICCAI 2026; an extended radiative Gaussian registration framework, RadGS-Reg+, was published online in Medical Image Analysis.

Qinghua Li, Junfeng Jiang*, Xueze Zhang, et al.
DHN-Seg: Expert-Guided Neural Tissue Segmentation in Individual Frames of Endoscopic Spine Videos
ISPCT 2026 · Accepted

Image semantic segmentation (ISS) of neural tissue in independently processed endoscopic frames. Expert-defined difficulty categories guide challenging-nerve pixel mining and nerve-mimicking distractor suppression, with category-specific evaluation alongside overall segmentation metrics.

Project repository

Code release details to be announced. Patient images, videos, annotations, and the dataset are not publicly released due to ethical and data-sharing restrictions. Not clinically validated.

Ao Shen, Junfeng Jiang*, Xueming Fu, Qiang Zeng, Ye Tang, Zhengming Chen, Luming Nong, Feng Wang, Shaohua Kevin Zhou*
FlowReg: Reconstruction-Guided Flow Matching for Universal Spinal CT/X-Ray Registration
MICCAI 2026 · Published online 2026-09-25 · LNCS 16889, pp. 77–87
DOI Official paper Repository

FlowReg combines biplanar X-ray reconstruction with flow-based pose estimation for both single- and multi-vertebra CT/X-ray registration. Reconstructed volumes provide spatial guidance, while SE(3)-aware supervision and difference-aware feature fusion support pose learning and cross-modal alignment.

* Joint corresponding authors. Code forthcoming.

RadGS-Reg+: Teacher-Student Radiative Gaussian Reconstruction and 3D/3D Registration for Lumbar CT/X-ray Registration
Junfeng Jiang†*, Ao Shen†, Zhan Wang, Xueming Fu, Zhengming Chen, Luming Nong, Feng Wang, Shaohua Kevin Zhou*
Medical Image Analysis · Online 2026 · Volume 115 (2027), Article 104319
DOI

RadGS-Reg+ reconstructs a registration-oriented vertebral representation from biplanar X-rays and aligns it with preoperative CT in 3D. Building on RadGS-Reg, it adds CT-to-X-ray teacher–student distillation, adaptive biplanar feature fusion, and discrepancy-aware pose regression.

† Joint first authors. * Joint corresponding authors.

RadGS-Reg: Registering Spine CT with Biplanar X-Rays via Joint 3D Radiative Gaussians Reconstruction and 3D/3D Registration
MICCAI 2025 · LNCS 15968, pp. 456–466

Paper DOI Official paper Project Code

Jointly reconstructs a 3D radiative Gaussian representation from biplanar X-rays and registers it with preoperative CT. Counterfactual attention focuses learning on vertebral regions, while patient-specific pretraining supports adaptation from simulated to intraoperative images.

Software

XBone Studio — An Integrated CT/MR Research Workstation

Research prototype / Internal evaluation · v0.11.104

XBone Studio is a Windows-based workstation for research and teaching, bringing together CT/MR visualization, segmentation editing, 3D reconstruction, and registration. It supports linked multiplanar views, interactive mask editing with live surface previews, 3D region marking, and landmark-based and ICP surface registration.
